# Barkpark Studio · Thursday 19 November 2026

Thirty-three pull requests merged. Fourteen change what an editor sees: three for one-line rich text, five for fields by role, four for two people in one rich-text block, one for arrays at their maximum and one for live updates. Four tighten the tests that read the source around the field rule. Eleven are the repo's daily jobs and their tests, three are end-to-end test upkeep, and one was the previous day's News ([#1295](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1295)).

## What a person can now do

**One-line rich text** ([#1291](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1291)). For a dek, a caption or a heading that needs marks and links but never a second paragraph. Sanity puts `oneLine` on the array's `block` member; here it sits on the rich text field, beside its other options.

1. Give a Portable Text field `"options": {"oneLine": true}`. The box is one line tall, with no drag corner.
2. Press Enter or Shift+Enter: no line breaks. Enter over a selection deletes it.
3. Paste several paragraphs, as plain text or HTML. They join into the block the caret is in.
4. Open Insert. It offers no block object; inline objects stay. A pasted image no longer becomes an image block.
5. Open a stored value that already holds several blocks, such as an import. It opens as it is and is never rewritten. Enter adds no block, and Backspace at a line's start joins the lines.

A screen reader hears a one-line text box ([#1302](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1302)). Text put in with a line break, by dictation, a text service or autocorrect, lands with one space for each run of breaks. Text that arrives the way the editor sends it on Android lands with a space where each break was, instead of running the words together ([#1304](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1304)).

**Fields hidden or read-only by role** ([#1288](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1288)). A studio's config sets Sanity's `hidden` and `readOnly` callbacks under `form.conditions`, keyed by type and field, for example `post.stage`. The callback gets Sanity's `currentUser`, with Sanity's role names: owner and admin are `administrator`, member is `editor`, contributor is `contributor`, and a seat that can't write is `viewer`. With shared sign-in it is `null`.

1. The rule works on top-level fields, fields inside objects and fields in array items. A group tab goes away when every field in it is hidden.
2. A read-only field takes no typing, list edit, rich-text edit or fix-it-card button.
3. Paste document and Paste field never write a field that is hidden from you but shown to another role, at any depth. Sanity's paste checks top-level read-only fields only. Paste document names the top-level read-only fields it skipped in a toast.
4. That holds for a field shown only to a custom role you lack, such as `reviewer`. The studio reads the workspace's role names from Barkpark with your own token. If Barkpark does not answer, it compares the built-in roles ([#1306](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1306)).
5. A callback that throws, or answers anything but `true`, leaves the field shown and editable, as in Sanity.

The docs say it plainly: this is a form convenience, not access control. No rule ships in the default config.

**One rule on every surface** ([#1301](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1301), [#1298](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1298)). The form, both pastes, the Freeform canvas and the save ask the same rule.

- The Freeform canvas is read-only while any field it holds is read-only for you, and is not drawn while one is hidden from you. "Edit the body in Freeform" on a read-only body opens a read-only canvas.
- A change by hand to a field read-only for you is not saved, and a toast names the field.
- On a clash in such a field, the card offers the other editor's version and shows your unsaved text with **Copy mine**, in place of **Keep mine**.
- Unsaved changes kept in this browser go back only into fields you may edit. The rest stay kept, and the pane names them.
- A success message shows only on success. The Translations menu says "Set document language" only when the language was set.
- A document action still writes a field the form shows read-only, as Sanity's actions do.

**Fields stay closed until Studio knows your role** ([#1312](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1312)). Until Barkpark has answered who you are, every field with a role rule is closed to typing, pastes and the save, and a field a rule may hide is hidden. When that check fails or has not answered in 10 s, the document says so, names any kept edits with their date, and offers **Retry**. A retry that answers opens what you may edit and puts kept edits back. The notice shows only on a document whose type has a role rule or has kept edits waiting. The cost, only for a studio that adds role rules: those fields stay closed for one round trip on each page load. English and Norwegian.

**Two people in one rich-text block** ([#1297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1297), [#1310](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1310), [#1314](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1314)). Open one document in two browsers and work in the same paragraph.

1. Both paste into it, at once or a second apart. Each paste is stored once, either save order stores the same text, and both screens show what is stored. A heading or list style the other set on the block is kept. The same text pasted at the same place by both is stored once.
2. One keeps typing while the other's paste splits the block. Every word is stored once.
3. One deletes letters while the other's paste splits the block. The deleted letters stay deleted.
4. One deletes a letter while the other deletes the block break next to it and pastes or splits there. The letter stays deleted when it is still in its own block. Where the merge cannot tell a moved letter from a new one, the letter is kept.

In a seeded test of 3,000 random edit pairs from both sides, merged in both orders, no letter was lost or stored twice and the two orders never differed ([#1297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1297)).

**Restore after a reload** ([#1318](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1318)). Editor A pastes a line, and A's page reloads while the save is still on its way. Meanwhile B pastes paragraphs at the start of the same block. When A presses **Restore**, both texts are kept, each once. When Restore cannot tell where the kept edit belongs, it writes nothing, the edit stays kept and the card stays up.

**Arrays at their maximum** ([#1292](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1292)). In object, string and reference arrays with `Rule.max(n)` and n items or more:

1. **Add item** is disabled. Hovering it shows "Maximum items reached" (Norwegian too).
2. The item menu's **Duplicate**, **Add item before** and **Add item after** are off. **Remove** and **Copy** stay.
3. **Paste field** stays. A paste that would add past the max is refused with a toast; one that replaces the array with one at or under the max goes in.
4. A file dropped on a full image array is refused and nothing is written. Below the max, a drop of several files takes the ones that fit and says so.
5. Remove an item and adding comes back, live in a second editor too. An array already past its max keeps every item.

Sanity does steps 1 and 2 for Add item and Add before/after; here Duplicate, paste and drops count too. A max at warning level counts, as in Sanity. The tags layout, checkbox lists and Portable Text are not affected.

**Live updates after a dropped connection** ([#1299](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1299)). When the studio server's connection to Barkpark's live stream drops while it is catching up after a reconnect, it now asks again from the last change it actually received. Open pages get the newest changes. The two live tests ran 20 times each on all seven CI lanes: 280 of 280 passed.

**Harder to break.** Four PRs widen the tests that read the source and fail when code writes to a field past the rule. They check which files may hold a writer, which names each may export, and that the pastes read the roles themselves with your own token ([#1309](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1309), [#1311](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1311), [#1313](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1313), [#1316](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1316)).

**Tooling.** Once a day a job compares the studio's PortableDoc conformance pack (the shared test cases for rendering Barkpark's documents) with Barkpark's. On a real change it opens one sync PR, which lands once every check is green, and it takes only plain files ([#1286](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1286), [#1305](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1305)). The first sync is in [#1286](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1286); the renderer's result is unchanged. The daily jobs that push to the repo hold their push key only in the step that pushes, and tests check that ([#1290](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1290), [#1293](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1293), [#1294](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1294), [#1300](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1300), [#1303](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1303)). Those tests got faster: 7.3 to 2.4 s on CI ([#1296](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1296)), and about 22 to 6-8 s on a Mac ([#1315](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1315)), with git's start-up on a Mac trimmed further ([#1317](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1317)). They no longer fail now and then on CI's newer git ([#1319](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1319)). End-to-end: two specs put a test document back only after a test changed it, about 1 s a run ([#1308](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1308)); the recorded test times went from 348.2 to 339.9 s, and the 7 test groups plan about 48 s each ([#1323](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1323)); and the test proxy sends `/vision` to the desk's Vision (the desk is the studio Barkpark draws itself) ([#1321](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1321)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- None of today's screens was compared side by side with a running Sanity Studio. One-line and arrays were built from Sanity's source; the reference studio has no role-based field ([#1291](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1291), [#1292](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1292), [#1288](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1288)).
- One-line fields, array maximums and role rules have no end-to-end test in CI. CI's datasets lack such fields until they are reseeded, and CI uses shared sign-in, so no role can be set. No real signed-in session tried the role rules; the roles read was tested against fakes only ([#1291](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1291), [#1292](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1292), [#1288](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1288), [#1306](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1306)).
- Android was read from the editor's source, not tried on a device. A lone arriving line break right before a space gives two spaces ([#1304](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1304)).
- With no answer at all from the role check, the notice took about 24 s to show on a dev build ([#1312](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1312)).
- Two people in one block, still open: after the other's split, your caret stays in the first piece, so your next words go there (each still stored once) ([#1310](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1310)). A link across the paste point is dropped when the paste saves second. Undo of a paste that split a block also takes out the other person's words merged into it ([#1297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1297)). In a test of 4,000 random edit pairs, 327 still merge wrong, as many as before this change ([#1314](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1314)).
- When Restore cannot tell where a kept edit belongs, the card stays up but does not show the kept text yet. A paste the editor has not handed on yet, up to about a second, is not kept if the page reloads in that gap ([#1318](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1318)).
- A typing save whose answer from Barkpark differs from what was sent now makes one extra history read ([#1310](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1310)).
- The key tests' goal of under 5 s on a Mac is not met ([#1315](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1315)). One test group ran 57-59 s against its 49.7 s plan and hit its cap 3 times in an hour before the times were re-recorded ([#1323](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1323)).
- With `/vision` mapped, the saved-query Vision tests open the desk's Vision and stop at what it lacks yet: a dataset picker, Params and saved queries ([#1321](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1321)).
