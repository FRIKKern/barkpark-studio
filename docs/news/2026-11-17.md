# Barkpark Studio · Tuesday 17 November 2026

Six pull requests merged. Five add parts of Sanity's form that a schema could name but that did nothing here. One started this News ([#1272](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1272)).

## What an editor can now do

**A table field** ([#1260](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1260)). A field declared as Sanity's `@sanity/table` is stored in that plugin's shape, so a Sanity dataset's tables open here and ours open there.

1. Press **Create Table** for a 2 × 2 grid. Add rows and columns, or many at once from the table menu; every removal asks first.
2. Move with Tab, Enter and the arrow keys. Each row and column header is a menu: insert, move, remove.
3. Paste from Excel, Sheets, Numbers or a CSV into a cell. The paste fills from that cell and grows the table as it needs.
4. With two editors in one table, text in different cells is merged cell by cell. When the other editor adds or removes a column, removes your row or writes the same cell while your text is not saved yet, theirs is the table and your text waits whole on a card under it, with Copy, Put it back and Dismiss. The card survives a reload.

A read-only seat, the Published view and a past revision show the same grid with no menus and write nothing.

**Time zones on a datetime** ([#1273](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1273)). Give a datetime field `options.displayTimeZone`, for example `America/New_York`. It shows and takes its time on that clock, wherever you sit, with a zone button after the field's title. Unless `allowTimeZoneSwitch: false`, the button opens **Select time zone**: search a city or an offset, or pick your local zone. The pick changes only how this field in this document shows its time, is remembered across reloads and, when you sign in with an account, on your other browsers. It never changes the stored value. Fields without these options, and date-only fields, are unchanged.

**More validation rules** ([#1274](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1274)). Rules written as Sanity's now mark the field as you type, in Sanity's words in English and Norwegian, and list in the validation panel: exact length, precision, greater than and less than, positive and negative, lowercase and uppercase, named and inverted patterns, a date's min and max, and an image or file with no asset. An error blocks Publish; a warning or info does not.

**Deprecated fields** ([#1275](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1275)). A field marked `deprecated: {reason}` shows a "deprecated" badge after its title and the reason under it. It keeps its input and its value, as in Sanity. A deprecated Portable Text block or inline type stays in Insert but is greyed out, with the reason as its tooltip.

**Array options** ([#1271](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1271)). `options.disableActions` takes the named actions (add, add before, add after, remove, duplicate, copy) out of the item menu, the Add item button, Copy and Paste field and their keys. `sortable: false` removes the drag handles. `modal: {type: 'popover'}` edits an object item in a popover by its row instead of a dialog.

## Where it is slow or rough

- None of the five was compared side by side with a running Sanity Studio: four were matched from Sanity's source and the table from the @sanity/table package ([#1260](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1260), [#1271](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1271), [#1273](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1273), [#1274](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1274), [#1275](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1275)).
- A table over 60 rows draws only the rows in view. A 200 × 20 table opens in about 2 s ([#1260](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1260)).
- The table went through 11 rounds of fixes before it merged. Its random two-editor test, at 2,000 scenes a seed, shows 0 lost, split or doubled text on every seed set; its last unit run took 26.1 s ([#1260](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1260)).

## Across the projects

Barkpark checks a table against a named object (`"type": "composite", "namedType": "table"`), so the table needed no Barkpark change ([#1260](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1260)). The zone pick is saved in Barkpark's user prefs (`/v1/prefs`) ([#1273](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark-studio/pull/1273)). The same day Barkpark's own editor, the desk, got a Validation panel that keeps Publish off while an error blocks, and a menu on each array item, both copied from this Studio ([FRIKKern/barkpark#26685](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/26685), [FRIKKern/barkpark#26813](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/26813)).
